**AspiraFi: Mindful Digital Spending Framework
**Beyond the Tap: Understanding Digital Spending Behavior and Promoting Mindful Financial Decision-Making Among Young Adults
📌** About the Project
*AspiraFi is a behavioral finance project that explores how digital payment platforms influence spending behavior, financial awareness, and impulsive purchasing decisions among young adults.

*The project combines primary survey research, statistical analysis, behavioral finance concepts, and practical fintech recommendations to encourage mindful spending in the digital economy.

🎯**Project Objectives
*Understand digital spending behavior among young adults.
*Analyze the relationship between digital payments and impulsive spending.
*Examine the influence of financial awareness on spending patterns.
*Study behavioral finance concepts such as the "Pain of Paying".
*Develop the AspiraFi framework to promote mindful financial decision-making.

🔍** Key Findings
*Frequent use of digital payments increases impulsive spending tendencies.
*Financial awareness is negatively associated with overspending.
*Convenience reduces spending consciousness.
*Emotional triggers significantly affect financial decisions.
*Respondents preferred goal-based reminders and spending-awareness tools.
🚀 **AspiraFi Framework
*The AspiraFi Framework recommends:

✅* Spending Awareness Scores

✅* Goal-Based Financial Nudges

✅* Cash-Like Visualization of Digital Spending

✅* Emotional Spending Alerts

✅ *Gamified Savings Challenges

The framework aims to help young adults balance lifestyle aspirations with financial discipline.
